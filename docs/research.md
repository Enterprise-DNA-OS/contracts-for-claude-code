# ContractSafe target research

Checked 8 October 2026. Family: contract-management, previously absent from the rebuild registry. Industry hub: professional-firms. This build supports the replace-your-software funnel and an owner's contract Command Centre.

## Bill first

The current [vendor pricing page](https://www.contractsafe.com/pricing) carries its volume-band rates in the HTML selector. Maximize for 1,001-2,500 contracts is US$1,325 per month prepaid annually: US$15,900 for twelve months. At 5,001-10,000 it is US$1,825 per month prepaid annually. All plans include unlimited users. Annual prepayment and monthly billing have different rates. USD is the billing currency. Do not use the older downloadable pricing PDF as the current rate.

These are public list-price scenarios, not verified NZ or AU customer invoices. Local customer Markdown contained no confirmed ContractSafe account. No live CRM access or customer subscription was verified. Australian data residency appears on the vendor pricing page; that is product availability, not proof of an existing buyer.

## The comparison claim we can support

ContractSafe already supports reminders, reporting and plain-language search. Its [Smart Search help](https://www.contractsafe.com/support/ai-powered-natural-launguage-search) currently says date-reminder filtering is not supported in natural-language search. The free version's reminders-due report directly answers that record question. The ten demo questions are working analyses, not assertions that ContractSafe has no way to answer all of them.

## Export evidence

[Contracts List](https://www.contractsafe.com/support/how-do-i-customize-my-contracts-list) describes CSV export. [Standard fields](https://www.contractsafe.com/support/standard-fields-in-contractsafe) distinguishes end date, nonrenewal deadline and contract value. [Downloads](https://www.contractsafe.com/support/how-do-i-download-selected-contracts-and-data) describes original file and bulk exports. See the replace guide for mapping and exclusions.

## Scope

The base is post-signature administration: renewal decisions, obligations, reminders, record checks, evidence references and internal paperwork. Signing and clause interpretation are not recreated. No regulatory certification, savings guarantee or unauthenticated approval claim is made.
